## Preparation

Drag a slice of lime around the rim of the glass. Place a mixture of equal parts Tajin and sea salt onto a plate. Coat the rim of the glass with the mixture by rolling it around the plate. 

Fill a pilsner glass or tall glass with ice.

## Assembling the Drink

Carefully pour a bottle of beer into the glass, avoiding too much head. Add the rest of the ingredients and gently stir.

## Garnish

The Michelada is known for being served with outrageous garnishes, such as sausages, pickled vegetables, bacon, shrimp, and lobster tails, often all together. Get creative and go as crazy as you want with garnish on this refreshing summer drink.

## Tips & Tricks

For the beer, try a pilsner-style lager or dark lager, like Modelo Especial or Modelo Negra.
