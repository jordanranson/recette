## Preparation

Fill a tumbler with ice.

## Assembling the Drink

Put a scoop of ice into a cocktail shaker and add ingredients except for the red wine. Shake vigorously for 20 seconds. Strain into the tumbler. Float the red wine by gently pouring it over the back of a spoon into the drink.
