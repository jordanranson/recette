## Preparation

Chill a coupe (optionally, rim with sugar). Set aside while preparing the drink.

## Assembling the Drink

Add all ingredients to a shaker without ice and dry shake for about 20 seconds to emulsify and fluff egg whites, then add ice to the shaker and shake well. Strain into your chilled coupe and garnish with a lemon twist.

## Making Earl Grey-Infused Gin

Add approximately 2tbsp (approx. 3 tea bags) of earl grey tea per 375mL of London dry gin. Cover and let sit at room temperature for 2-4 hours, lightly agitating every hour or so, before removing tea bags (if using loose tea, strain to remove tea leaves).

Tanqueray was typically used for the infusion, but any London dry should produce good results.
