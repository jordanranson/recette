## Preparation

Place one large ice cube in a tumbler.

## Assembling the Drink

Assemble the ingredients in the shaker, without ice. Shake vigorously for 20 seconds. Add ice and shake again for another 20 seconds. Strain into the tumbler. Garnish with dash of bitters on top of the foam.
