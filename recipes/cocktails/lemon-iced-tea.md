## Preparation

Bring water to a boil. Add the tea bags and let steep for 5 minutes. Remove the tea bags and mix in the simple syrup. Finally, add the ice and lemon juice then stir for several minutes.

## Serving

Pour over ice and garnish with a lemon wheel.

## Tips & Tricks

Adding the lemon juice at the end with the ice helps prevent it from heating up which dulls the flavour.
