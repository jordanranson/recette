## Preparation

Before adding other ingredients, stir up coconut milk to re-emulsify any separated solids. Finely grate ginger and garlic on a microplane and juice limes. Combine all ingredients and add to your meats.

## Marinating recommendations

For tougher cuts of steak like sirloin tips or skirt steaks, allow to marinate for between 6-8 hours. Chicken can be left for between 2-6 hours, while shrimp only take around 30-60 minutes.

A single batch of marinade should be enough for approximately 8-10 chicken thighs/breasts or 3-4 ~8oz steaks with enough left over for ~4 servings of rice (see tips and tricks below)

## Tips & Tricks

Don't discard leftover marinade! Pour remaining marinade into a pot and bring to a boil to kill any bacteria from the meats, then simmer to reduce. It goes great on rice, or just add to the finished meat for extra flavour.

If you dig cilantro, sprinkle it over the finished meats and rice.
